| Nyckel | Engelska | Sparad svenska |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `component::vesync::entity::sensor::cook_end_time_basket::name` | Basket {basket} cooking end | Tillagningen avslutas för korg {basket} |
| `component::vesync::entity::sensor::cook_end_time::name` | Cooking end | Tillagningen avslutas |
